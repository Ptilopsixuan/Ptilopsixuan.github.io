## Research Experience

### Development and Testing of a Buckling-Free Low-Prestressed Self-Centering Brace

- Supervisor: Prof. Ying Zhou, DDMS, College of Civil Engineering, Tongji University
- Designed and tested a Buckling-Free Low-Prestressed Self-Centering Brace (BF-LPSC Brace) to reduce prestress demand and avoid compression-induced buckling. Supported full-scale prototype fabrication and experimental testing under static, dynamic, and fatigue loading protocols. Calibrated an OpenSees model with experimental data, confirming stable flag-shaped hysteresis and reliable self-centering behavior.

### Unity-Based Digital Twin Visualization Tool for Seismic Response of Building Clusters

- Supervisor: Prof. Ying Zhou, DDMS, College of Civil Engineering, Tongji University
- Built an interactive Unity-based visualization tool for structural and seismic response data at the building-cluster scale, integrating numerical model outputs and earthquake response records to support clearer interpretation of structural performance during scenario earthquakes.

### Field Data Collection Support for Facade Degradation and Maintenance Monitoring

- Supervisor: Prof. Wensheng Lu, DDMS, College of Civil Engineering, Tongji University
- Collected and coded campus facade-condition observations, including discoloration, cracking, detachment, and post-maintenance states. Organized field records to support subsequent monitoring of facade deterioration and evaluation of repair performance.

### Quasi-Static Test on Carbon-Fiber-Reinforced Concrete Frame

- Participated in on-site quasi-static testing of a carbon-fiber-reinforced concrete frame. Assisted with test setup and loading process observation, recorded crack development and damage progression, and organized experimental observations for subsequent structural performance analysis.

## Engineering Project Experience

### Huadian Kashgar CHP Plant Seismic Resilience Enhancement

- Supported seismic resilience enhancement for a lifeline-infrastructure combined heat and power (CHP) project. Assessed the seismic performance of the main turbine hall, identified resilience gaps in the existing structural scheme, and proposed a retrofit solution using self-centering braces. Implemented a customized brace constitutive model in commercial finite element software, and participated in engineering and productization design to support practical deployment.

### Xiong'an New Area Building-Cluster Seismic Resilience Evaluation

### Building Safety Inspection for Weifang Elderly Care Home, Pudong New Area

- Conducted on-site building safety inspection by measuring overall building dimensions and comparing them with design drawings. Performed settlement observation and inclination measurement for the building, and tested the strength of beams, columns, and masonry components.
